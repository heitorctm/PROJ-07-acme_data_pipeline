# OPCH — A/P Invoice (header)

**SAP table:** OPCH  
**Bronze name:** purchase_invoice_opch  
**Total raw columns:** 23

## Description

Stores the headers of A/P invoices (A/P Invoice in SAP B1). Each row represents an invoice received from a vendor. It is the document that records the receipt of goods or services into the company's liabilities, generated from a purchase order (OPOR) or directly.

## Relationships

**Child tables — join on `DocEntry`:**
- PCH1 (`purchase_invoice_lines_pch1`) — item rows of the A/P invoice
- PCH6 (`purchase_invoice_installments_pch6`) — payment installments of the A/P invoice
- PCH12 (`purchase_invoice_usage_pch12`) — tax usage extension of the A/P invoice

**Parent tables — OPCH references:**
- OBPL via `BPLId` — issuing branch

**Traceability (via PCH1):**
- OPOR via `PCH1.BaseEntry` where `PCH1.BaseType = 22` — purchase order that originated the A/P invoice

## Database notes

- 72,450 A/P invoices in total; join with PCH1 confirmed for 72,449 documents
- `DocStatus = 'D'` with only 2 records — not in the SDK; log and monitor
- `TaxDate` populated in only 305 of 72,755 records — tax accrual date, different from `DocDate` (posting date)
- `TransId` allows a direct join with `OJDT.TransId` to trace the journal entry generated by the A/P invoice
