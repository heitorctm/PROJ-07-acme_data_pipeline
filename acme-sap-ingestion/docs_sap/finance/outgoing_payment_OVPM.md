# OVPM — Outgoing Payment

**SAP table:** OVPM  
**Bronze name:** outgoing_payment_ovpm  
**Total raw columns:** 10

## Description

Stores payments to vendors and other payees (Outgoing Payments in SAP B1). Each row represents a payment made — linked to A/P invoices (OPCH), journal entries (OJDT) and the receiving partner. The `TransId` field links the payment to its corresponding journal entry in OJDT.

OVPM has no `UpdateTS` in raw — the watermark has daily precision.

## Relationships

**Parent tables — OVPM references:**
- OCRD via `CardCode` — vendor or customer who received the payment
- OBPL via `BPLId` — branch that made the payment
- OJDT via `TransId` — journal entry generated by the payment
